Our story

Korean flavour, local roots.

Momo Fried Chicken was built on the idea that Korean fried chicken deserved a proper home in Australia. Not a watered-down version — the real thing, made with the right techniques and the right ingredients.

Our menu pulls from Korean street food culture — toppoki, cupbap, bulgogi soups, and fried chicken that actually crunches. Everything is made fresh in-house. We don't believe in holding food that should be served hot.
